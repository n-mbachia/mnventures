About This Guide

Most Kenyan mushroom farmers fail not because mushrooms are hard to grow — but because no one showed them the full picture: how to grow consistently, who to sell to, and how to run it as a real business.

From the Bag to the Bank closes that gap. Written for first-time and early-stage Kenyan farmers, it walks you through every step — from choosing your first spawn to signing your first hotel supply agreement — in plain, practical language.

Inside, you will learn how to avoid the contamination mistakes that destroy 30% of most first batches, how to sell to hotels, supermarkets, and online buyers, and how to multiply your income by drying and powdering your harvest. A complete KES 100,000 financial case study shows you exactly what to expect — costs, revenue, cashflow, and a 235% annual return when managed correctly.

5 Reasons to Read This Guide

1
Stop losing crops to contamination
A full biosafety chapter covers 5 pests, 6 diseases, and a step-by-step clean-room protocol — protecting the investment that most first-time farmers lose in their first two cycles.
2
Know exactly what you'll earn before you start
A live KES 100,000 case study shows your monthly cashflow, break-even in Month 4, and 235% annual ROI. No guesswork — just clear numbers.
3
Multiply your income without growing more mushrooms
Learn to dry and powder your harvest. The same crop that earns KES 550/kg fresh earns KES 4,000–8,000/kg dried — opening supermarket, export, and online health markets.
4
Walk into any buyer meeting prepared
A pricing strategy section teaches you your floor price, how to negotiate by customer type, and how to say no to a bad deal — professionally.
5
Turn farming into a registered, bankable business
Covers business registration, tax obligations, KEBS certification, and how to access SACCOs, KCB Agri-loans, and government funds — with your farm records as proof.
