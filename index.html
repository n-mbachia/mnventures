From Mushroom Bag to the Bank

The Complete Mushroom Farm Guide ebook

Most Kenyan mushroom farmers fail not because mushrooms are hard to grow — but because no one showed them the full picture: how to grow consistently, who to sell to, and how to run it as a real business. From the Bag to the Bank closes that gap. Written for firsttime and early-stage Kenyan farmers, it walks you through every step — from choosing your first spawn to signing your first hotel supply agreement — in plain, practical language. Inside, you will learn how to avoid the contamination mistakes that destroy 30% of most first batches, how to sell to hotels, supermarkets and online buyers, and how to multiply your income by drying and powdering your harvest. A complete KES 100,000 financial case study shows you exactly what to expect — costs, revenue, cashflow, and a 235% annual return of investment when managed correctly. This includes a bonus Excel spreadsheet that can be used to monitor and track your business mushroom from start to harvest and expansion.
